Privacy Policy

AMAR HATHAZARI

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

AMAR HATHAZARI (“we”, “our” or “the app”) is a local information and community-services application for Hathazari. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app and how it is used.

1. Information we may collect or process

  • Donor registration information: if you register as a blood donor, we process the name, phone number, blood group, area, last donation date (if provided), availability status and your public-contact preference that you submit.
  • Notification information: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may use a device registration token and notification settings to deliver app notifications.
  • Information you send to us: information you include when contacting the support email listed below.
  • App and service data: the app retrieves public service listings, notices, jobs, healthcare information and market listings from our backend. Our backend and service providers may process technical information needed to provide, secure and maintain these services.

Do not submit another person's personal information unless you have their permission.

2. How we use information

  • To receive and manage blood donor registrations.
  • To display donor details only when the registration has been approved and the donor has enabled public contact.
  • To send service announcements and alerts when notifications are enabled.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot and protect the app and its backend.
  • To respond to questions, correction requests and deletion requests.

3. Donor information and public visibility

Blood donor information is submitted for community donor services. A registration may remain pending until reviewed by an administrator. If approved and the public-contact option is enabled, relevant donor details may be shown to app users so they can contact the donor. Do not enable public contact unless you agree to this visibility. You may request correction or removal by emailing us.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase for backend/database and storage services and Google Firebase for push notifications. The app may open external services such as Google Maps, WhatsApp, phone or email applications when you choose those features. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information. We do not share donor contact details for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the relevant service, meet legitimate operational requirements and resolve disputes. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted by emailing developeriftekhar21@gmail.com. Please identify the app and the information concerned. We may need to verify the request before acting. Some records may be retained where required for security, legal or administrative reasons.

6. Security

We use access controls and service-provider security features intended to protect information. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s privacy

AMAR HATHAZARI is a general community-services app and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Permissions and choices

Notification permission is optional and can be changed in your device settings. You can choose whether to submit a donor registration and whether to enable public contact on that registration. External apps open only when you choose the related feature.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date.
